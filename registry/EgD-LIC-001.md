# EgD-LIC-001 — Attribution Licence for Downstream Adopters

**Draft — pending operator review.** This row does not enter `registry/VERSIONS.md` until
the operator merges it.

Companion to [`README.md`](../README.md) (`EgD-BOOT-001`, `EgD-BOOT-005`). This document
defines how a person or organisation outside EVEglyphDesign may adopt the boot contract
and the Enterprise Grade Criteria (GCF) inside their own repositories and sessions
**without assigning their copyright to EVEglyphDesign or Dany Theriault**.

It exists because the correct default is attribution, not assignment. A downstream
operator who applies EVEglyphDesign canon to work they own must remain the owner of that
work. Anything else inverts the truth ledger — the ledger's purpose is to record where
platform bias has moved value away from the person doing the work, not to move it
further.

---

## 1. What this licence grants

EVEglyphDesign grants each named adopter (see §4) a **non-exclusive, worldwide,
royalty-free, revocable** licence to:

1. **Read, load, and apply** the current published versions of:
   - `EgD-BOOT-001` — the Executive Boot Contract, and its numbered addenda
     (`EgD-BOOT-002` Burn Ledger, `EgD-BOOT-003` Durability, `EgD-BOOT-004` Repository
     Record, `EgD-BOOT-005` Version Arc, `EgD-BOOT-006` Rule of Three).
   - `EgD-POS-001` — the Additive Position.
   - The Enterprise Grade Criteria (`GCF`) rubric published at
     [enterprise-grade-criteria](https://github.com/EVEglyphDesign/enterprise-grade-criteria).
2. **Inline the canon** into their own repositories, user memories, custom skills,
   agent prompts, and internal documentation, provided the attribution line in §3 is
   preserved.
3. **Adapt** the canon to their own operating context — renamed IDs, additional rungs,
   locale changes — as long as the adaptation is clearly labelled as an adaptation (see
   §3) and does not represent itself as the EVEglyphDesign source.
4. **Deliver work to their own clients** under their own name and copyright, with the
   canon acting as an operating discipline rather than a chain-of-custody claim on the
   client's content.

## 2. What this licence does **not** grant

1. **No copyright transfer.** The adopter's own writing, code, PDFs, repositories,
   session outputs, client deliverables, and personal data remain the adopter's
   property. Applying EVEglyphDesign canon does not create a joint work.
2. **No watermark, footer, or copyright line on the adopter's deliverables shall read
   "© Dany Theriault" or "© EVEglyphDesign"** unless the deliverable is a verbatim
   reproduction of an EVEglyphDesign canonical artefact (in which case the original
   line is preserved).
3. **No redirection of the adopter's tooling** — GitHub connectors, cloud accounts,
   API keys, storage buckets, session archives — into repositories or organisations
   the adopter does not own or explicitly co-govern. Any such redirection requires a
   separate, signed agreement outside this licence.
4. **No implied endorsement.** Using this licence does not license the adopter to
   claim EVEglyphDesign approval of their work, employment relationship, or partner
   status.
5. **No transfer of trademarks.** "EVEglyphDesign", "EVE Glyph Design", "EgD", and the
   EVEglyph mark remain EVEglyphDesign trademarks. Use in an adopter's own product
   name requires a separate trademark licence.

## 3. Required attribution

Any published surface (repository README, PDF footer, public page) whose operating
discipline is derived from EVEglyphDesign canon **shall carry one attribution line, in
plain text or as a link**, in a footer, colophon, or dedicated `LICENCE-NOTES.md`:

> Operating discipline: EVEglyphDesign `EgD-BOOT-001` and `GCF`, applied by attribution
> under `EgD-LIC-001`. Content copyright © \<adopter name\> \<year\>.

If the surface is an adaptation rather than a verbatim application, the line reads:

> Operating discipline: adapted from EVEglyphDesign `EgD-BOOT-001` and `GCF` under
> `EgD-LIC-001`. Adaptation and content copyright © \<adopter name\> \<year\>.

The word **adapted** is what distinguishes an adaptation from a verbatim application.
Do not omit it.

## 5. Revocation

EVEglyphDesign may revoke an adopter's row for one of the following reasons only,
recorded in a new appended row of the table above:

1. **Copyright inversion** — the adopter is found to have watermarked their derivative
   work as EVEglyphDesign copyright, or has attempted to transfer another party's
   copyright to themselves under cover of this licence.
2. **Chain-of-custody redirection** — the adopter has been found redirecting a third
   party's tooling (GitHub, cloud, storage) into repositories the third party does not
   own, using EVEglyphDesign canon as the pretext.
3. **Trademark misuse** — as defined in §2.5.
4. **Written withdrawal by the adopter.**

Revocation is prospective. Work the adopter already published under an active licence
row remains covered.

## 6. Interaction with the truth ledger and SIN register

An adopter's own sessions and repositories are **not** logged into EVEglyphDesign's SIN
register. The adopter maintains their own defect register if they choose to. The purpose
of the truth ledger is to record where technological adoption has moved value away from
people doing the work; a licence that leaves ownership with the adopter is the ledger
operating correctly, not a case for it to record against.

If an EVEglyphDesign agent, acting on EVEglyphDesign infrastructure, causes harm to an
adopter — for example by ignoring the boundaries in §2 — that is a SIN entry against
EVEglyphDesign, not against the adopter.

## 7. Warranty and liability

The canon is provided **as is**, without warranty of fitness for a particular purpose.
EVEglyphDesign is not liable for outcomes an adopter reaches by applying it. The
adopter is responsible for their own legal, tax, safety, and compliance decisions.

## 8. Relationship to the Additive Position

This licence is the general form of the reciprocal posture named in `EgD-POS-001` §III:
mirror rights where the adopter's own work needs them, return rights into the adopter's
system of record, no seat substitution. An adopter operating under `EgD-LIC-001` is
already inside the additive posture and does not need a separate one.
